Never Drop: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

Publisher and controller: ClickPlay Games ("we", "us")

Contact: bgburakgunduz@gmail.com

Never Drop is a mobile game. You can play it offline. Buying optional items needs an internet connection. This policy explains what happens to information when you play and when you buy.

1. Summary

  • No account, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Ads: the game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch one) and, now and then, a short ad after a run, served by Google AdMob. Any purchase removes the after-run ads for good. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first. No analytics or crash-reporting service of our own. We do not track you across other apps or websites ourselves.
  • Your game progress stays on your phone.
  • If you buy an optional item, the purchase is handled by Google Play, and a purchase service called RevenueCat records which items you own so the game can unlock them and restore them.

2. What stays on your device

Your progress (scores, in-game coins called Sek, unlocked items, settings, language) is saved in files on your phone. We cannot see it and it is not uploaded. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. Cloud backup of this data is turned off in the app.

3. What is processed when you buy or restore purchases

  • Google Play (Google) processes your payment and holds your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store your card or payment details.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. ("RevenueCat") is a service provider (a "processor") that works on our behalf. The game sends it a random identifier created on your device when you first run the game (it is not linked to your name, email, Google account or advertising ID), together with the purchases made with that identifier, so that it can confirm what you own. RevenueCat also receives the usual technical information any internet request carries, such as your approximate region (country) derived from the request, the language and currency of your store, and the app version. RevenueCat describes how it handles data at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy .
  • Purchase history (which items you bought, when) is kept by us and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide your purchases and restore them on a new phone, to meet accounting and legal duties, and to handle refunds and disputes.
  • We do not sell this information and we do not use it for advertising. (Advertising data is covered in section 3a.)

3a. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show ads. Google acts as an independent ad provider. When an ad is requested, Google receives data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location is derived), device and app information, and ad interactions. Google uses it to choose and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and its ad-technology terms.
  • Consent: in the EEA, UK and Switzerland (and wherever the law requires), a Google consent form appears first. If you decline or the form cannot be shown, we do not request ads. You can change your choice later in the game's Settings (a privacy row appears only when it applies), and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings under Privacy > Ads.
  • The ad content rating is limited to PG. Ads never play during a run. Rewarded ads give a bonus only if you finish watching. The game is not directed to children under 13 and does not ask Google for child-directed treatment.
  • Retention and deletion of data Google holds is governed by Google; we cannot access it.

4. Permissions

The app uses: internet access and network status (to reach Google Play and RevenueCat when you buy or restore), Google Play billing (to make purchases), ad services and the advertising ID (to show ads, see 3a) and vibration (for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's settings or in Android). It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5. Children

Never Drop is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has made a purchase or that we hold a child's data, contact us at the address above and we will delete it.

6. Your choices and your rights

You can stop all data use by uninstalling the game; deleting the game data on your phone removes your local progress. To ask us to delete the purchase-related data we hold under your random identifier, or to ask for access to it, write to bgburakgunduz@gmail.com. We will ask RevenueCat to delete that customer record, which removes the data it holds for it. Purchases are also recorded by Google Play under your Google account; manage those through Google. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or Turkey) you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection and complaint to your data protection authority. Because we identify you only by a random identifier, we may ask you for that identifier or for your Google Play order number so that we can find your record.

Legal basis and transfers: we process purchase data to provide what you bought (performance of a contract) and to meet legal duties; advertising data is processed on the basis of your consent where the law requires it. RevenueCat and Google may process data in the United States and other countries under their own safeguards.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and publish the new text at this address. If a later version of the game adds features that use more data (for example online rankings), we will update this policy before those features are released.
