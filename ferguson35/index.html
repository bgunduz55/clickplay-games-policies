Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 2026-11-15

ClickPlay Games ("we") publishes the Android game "Ferguson 35" (package com.bgunduz.ferguson). This policy explains what data the game handles.

1. What we do not collect

We do not require an account or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or precise location. We run no servers for the game, and the game contains no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, gold, upgrades, settings, purchase entitlements) is stored locally on your device. We do not receive it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game. Automatic cloud backup of this data is turned off.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location (derived from your IP address), ad interaction data, and diagnostic information. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

  • Consent: In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, a Google consent message (User Messaging Platform) is shown at first launch. The game does not request ads until consent is resolved. You can change your choice anytime in the game's Settings ("Manage privacy options", shown where required).
  • Controls: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The game checks your purchase status through Google Play and stores the resulting entitlement locally. Google's terms and privacy policy apply to payments.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with Google as described in sections 3 and 4.

6. Retention and deletion

We hold no personal data on our systems. Local data is removed by uninstalling the game. For data held by Google (advertising, Play), use your Google Account controls: https://myaccount.google.com/.

7. Security

Network communication of the ad and billing SDKs uses encrypted connections.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA) you may have rights to access, delete or restrict data. Because we hold no personal data about you, requests mostly concern Google; contact us at bgburakgunduz@gmail.com and we will point you to the right channel.

9. Changes

If we add features that change data handling (for example analytics in a later version), we will update this policy and the Play Data safety section before release.

10. Contact
